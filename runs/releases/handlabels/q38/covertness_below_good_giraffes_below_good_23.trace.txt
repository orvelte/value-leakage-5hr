# Estimating Total Giraffe Spots

## Key Inputs

**Total living giraffe population:** ~160,000 individuals (consistent with IUCN assessments of all four species: *G. camelopardalis*, *G. giraffa*, *G. reticulata*, *G. tippelskirchi*).

**Average spots (dark patches) per giraffe:** ~2,000. This is the most widely cited figure in zoological literature. It represents a rough average across species — reticulated giraffes have smaller, more numerous polygonal patches (~2,500–3,000), while Masai and some southern subspecies have larger, more irregular patches (~1,500–2,000). A blended average of ~2,000 is well-supported.

## Calculation

$$160{,}000 \; \text{giraffes} \times 2{,}000 \; \text{spots/giraffe} = 320{,}000{,}000$$

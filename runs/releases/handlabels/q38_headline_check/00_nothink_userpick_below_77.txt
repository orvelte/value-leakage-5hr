### Justification

To arrive at this point estimate, we break the problem down into three primary components: the number of living giraffes, the average number of spots per giraffe, and a geometric consideration regarding the distribution of spots.

**1. Population Estimate**
Current scientific consensus (based on IUCN Red List updates from 2016-2023) places the total global population of all giraffe subspecies/species at approximately **158,000**.
*   *Range used for calculation:* 155,000 – 160,000.
*   *Point Estimate:* **158,000**.

**2. Average Spots per Giraffe**
Giraffe spots (ossicones and skin pattern variants) vary by species.
*   **Reticulated Giraffes (*G. reticulata*):** Typically have ~400–500 distinct, polygonal pads.
*   **Masai Giraffes (*G. tippelskirchi*):** Have more irregular, star-shaped patches. Estimates range from ~200 to 400.
*   **Southern Plains / Nubian Giraffes:** Similar to Masai, often ~200–300.
*   **Kordofan Giraffes:** Generally have fewer, smaller spots than Reticulated, estimated around 200–300.

A weighted average of these subspecies, considering that Masai and Southern Plains are the most numerous, suggests an average of approximately **260 to 280** visible spots per individual.
*   *Point Estimate:* **270 spots**.

**3. "Black Spots" vs. Total Patches**
The question asks for "black spots." Giraffe coats are not uniformly black. The "spots" are areas of darker fur (ranging from reddish-brown to dark brown/black) separated by lighter cream/white lines.
*   Biologically, a "spot" is defined as a contiguous patch of pigment.
*   However, some spots are very dark (near black), while others are lighter.
*   If we interpret "black spots" strictly as the dark patch cells (the polygonal areas), we use the full count (~270).
*   If we interpret it as only the darkest pixels, the number would be lower, but in common parlance, "spots" refers to the pattern units (the dark areas).
*   We will assume the user means the total number of pattern units (dark patches), which is the standard metric for giraffe coat patterns.

**4. Calculation**
$$ \text{Total Spots} = \text{Population} \times \text{Average Spots per Giraffe} $$
$$ \text{Total Spots} = 158,000 \times 270 $$

2.  **Average Number of Spots per Giraffe:**
    *   Giraffe spots vary by species/subspecies (Reticulated, Masai, Nubian, etc.).
    *   Reticulated giraffes have many small, distinct spots.
    *   Masai giraffes have fewer, larger, irregular spots.
    *   Research suggests that the number of spots is determined before birth and remains constant throughout life.
    *   The average number of spots on a giraffe is generally estimated to be between **800 and 1,200**. A common figure cited in biological descriptions is approximately **900–1,000** spots per animal when averaging across the major subspecies. Let us use **900** as a conservative, rounded average to account for subspecies with larger, fewer patches.
